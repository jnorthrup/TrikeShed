Context cleaner & breadcrumb trail

        
A rolling window of 50 turns records whether each outgoing prompt overflowed 100,000 estimated tokens. Under 2% overflow the cleaner scales records up and recalls dropped ones; over 5% it drops records older than a minute under 0.3 presence and scales the rest back by 10%. Dropped records keep their reference and stay retrievable.

        
The trail lists records in the order they were laid; a turn carries its tool results inside it. As presence decays each record holds fewer of its most distinct words. Recall moves a breadcrumb from 0, its reference, to 1, its original, and refreshes its recency.
