# Invented English/symbol corpus, 2026-11-04

This is public, invented fixture data: 12 semantic cases and four identity or
currentness controls, with 31 formal records. The English files contain 1,287
strict UTF-8 bytes; their KIF companions contain 1,425 bytes. Labels and
translations are declared synthetic assumptions, not expert legal ground truth.
No private excerpts, books or PDFs are included.

`manifest.json` uses the existing `provisional-legal-eval/1` contract. Each case
has one complete newly authored excerpt; original and excerpt identify the same
bytes. This makes no claim of extraction from several documents or authenticated
source lineage. The 30 annotated anchors use half-open UTF-16 code-unit offsets,
as do the English and KIF record spans in `input-declarations.json`.

`blind-requests.json` is the existing CLI's `prepare` output. It excludes expected
labels, anchors and the premise sidecar. `caseCid` in the declarations and sidecar
is the existing `LegalEvalCase.propositionCid`, emitted by that codec; it is not a
new identity format. Identities exclude labels and anchors. Keep the manifest and
gold sidecar away from a later prediction seat.

The sidecar keeps three lanes separate:

1. `identityAdmission` records expected identity or admission behavior. Actual
   admission, review, receipt, frame and source authority remain unobserved.
2. `typedProof` records the fragment, required ordered premise IDs, absent
   requirements, rule IDs and bindings. `inspectedRecordIds` includes distractors
   and context. `requiredPremiseIds` can describe a blocked attempted path; an
   absent entry is never a supplied assertion. All observed proof fields remain
   null. C1 records both valid single-premise alternatives. S12's two prerequisites
   describe weak induction, not a theorem path.
3. `recallHypothesisUnits` leaves the semantic seat MISSING. Admission coverage,
   resident coverage, recall, evidence units and bag answers have not been run.
   NAL's UNIT is 1,000 milli-evidence; attention and occurrence counts add no units.

Every record has exact English and KIF quotations, a declared role and a source
dependence group. English/KIF companions, repeated offsets, exports and replay
receipts do not become independent observations. Full declared term identities
govern entity equality; there is no subject-CID/source-CID equality fallback.
Resource and permission sort annotations are individual roles. Horn role positions
are explicit, but the positive Horn fragment does not enforce those annotations.

Manual KIF and the reviewed English carrier are different profiles. Bare manual
constants have global meaning in an explicitly selected theory; a deliberate
manual union can join them, and pure taxonomy does not enforce class/individual
disjointness or reject an approved punning IRI. The English carrier requires one
context, one current snapshot and consistent sorts, and refuses native/union
export. These JSON declarations are inert fixture assumptions, not a review,
carrier, parser adapter or permission to activate a protected plan. C4's two KIF
lines describe successive revisions; feeding both into one active theory would
discard the control's temporal meaning.

supplied controls are scorer behavior checks, not model performance or evidence
