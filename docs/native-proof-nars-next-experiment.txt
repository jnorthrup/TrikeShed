Recommendation
Use structure to select a bounded premise workset, not to mint confidence. First deliver a replayable, query-local class/role activation experiment with the live bag unchanged. A native proof is a deterministic derivation in its admitted positive fragment; recalling an existing signal or finding a matching sentence is not that derivation. The current upstream solver computes a forward closure: inspecting its returned proof afterward does not retroactively make its evaluation goal-driven or cheaper. To test goal-driven activation, select candidates BEFORE solving and compare selected versus complete-theory answers under the declared bounds.

Held-out semantic checks
- Positive conjunction: both roles and inherited class produce the held-out goal, whose exact tuple was never present in source facts. This distinguishes derivation from direct retrieval.
- Role swap: assignedTo(ProjectA,Alice), or authorization for ProjectB, must not produce eligibleFor(Alice,ProjectA) despite identical vocabulary.
- Missing premise: omit authorization; UNKNOWN/no proof, never false-world assertion. Add a near-synonym does not fill the missing exact relation.
- Duplicate path/source: two inheritance routes and two assertion records referencing the SAME source bytes do not change evidence/confidence. Native source alternatives are any-one; gather unique actual source leaves independently of how many times the proof references them. Different record IDs, inputIds or proofIds do not establish independent observations.
- First-proof limitation: reordering rules may change selected proof/rule IDs while status stays same. Freshly remove one support and rerun, then last support; old proof remains historical, not current authority. Do not infer all support disappeared merely because the first tree vanished.
- Scope: run analogous classification over genuine review-record metadata only, preserving worldObligationApplicability=UNKNOWN. No test promoting must/presumed or ontology alignment into world taxonomy.

What may later feed NARS
After this experiment, an explicitly authorized attention-only integration could map selected exact observation receipts to EXISTING bag cells via a new owner-side checked receipt/formula identity operation, then adjust budget without changing EvidenceCoord, basis or counted receipts. Verify byte-identical evidence before/after, repeated activations/restart idempotence, collision/missing-residency refusal and no unconfirmed-send=retained claim. A derived theorem should remain a proof receipt/read model first. Minting a new derived belief requires a separately reviewed evidence-unit/dependence/retraction policy; merely running the native solver is not a new independent source observation.

NAL induction/training remains separate
An inductive experiment needs disjoint observed examples and held-out outcomes, with frequency/confidence calibration and source dependence tracked. It predicts a hypothesis rather than proves a held-out Horn consequence. Neither proof-guided attention nor deterministic class/role deduction trains NAL by itself. No model confidence, similarity score or successful syntax/native lowering grants entailment or human approval.
