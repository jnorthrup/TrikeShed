The existing KgNalBridge.signal now carries an optional propositionCid. Its
canonical CBOR digest includes the complete retained lexical predicate, ordered
subject/object terms and available role CIDs, copula, relation, full available
temporal qualification, and DECLARED source context. Invalid UTF-16 is refused
before identity encoding. Confidence, evidence, budget, observation receipt and
Bloom hints are excluded. This digest identifies the declared projection; the
pure bridge does not store its canonical payload in CAS or authenticate it.
The current source-context parameter is the caller's sourceCid, so different
source content remains conservatively separate rather than automatically joined
as independent evidence. Identical source bytes can share a content context.
This is not authenticated document/jurisdiction context or cross-document entity
resolution. Existing Turtle lexical parsing still has limited RDF term-kind
fidelity; the new digest does not silently turn that parser into a full RDF model.

The owning bag compares known proposition IDs before same-receipt replay, source
basis revision, or resident/archive mutation. Different known IDs refuse with
PROPOSITION_MISMATCH; one known and one unknown ID refuse with
UNRESOLVED_PROPOSITION. Resident signal/evidence/gloss/budget and history remain
unchanged, and the worker continues. Exact matching IDs still require the existing
receipt and ancestry checks. Both-null legacy signals retain their current
subject/object/relation/temporal guard, with no full proposition-identity claim.
Reinforce preserves the resident identity through its existing guarded path.
Angular routing remains bounded attention; this repair refuses conflation rather
than retaining two distinct propositions in a single angular cell.

SignalCodec appends a twelfth field only when propositionCid is present. Null
signals retain identical eleven-field bytes and historical CAS identities. New
readers accept historical ten-field, eleven-field, and identified twelve-field
records; a ten-field read still has the pre-existing upgrade to an eleven-field
write. Old readers cannot read twelve fields. Adding the trailing optional Kotlin
data field preserves source construction defaults, not JVM binary compatibility:
all JVM consumers must rebuild together. CAS/WAL replay retains the field. The
older StateNodes freeze/thaw format already omits object/temporal qualification;
it also restores an unknown proposition identity. New known-identity revisions
therefore refuse rather than upgrading that history. This slice does not rewrite
legacy snapshots, live CAS/WAL history, or historical evidence.

The legacy KIF-to-triplet crossing now uses strict KifSemantic decoding with
whole-input bounds: 65536 UTF-16 characters, nesting64 and syntax nodes8192 total.
Only ground binary predicates with scalar constant/literal arguments cross.
Additional arguments, variables/functions, negation, modal/context operators,
implication/equivalence, quoted forms, malformed tails and oversized inputs refuse
the complete crossing. A valid prefix is not partially minted when another form
is unsupported. Full syntax and operators remain available to KifSemantic; they
are not interpreted as unconditional triplets. This deliberately narrows imports
through kg.ingest / nal.rules.fromKg. Explicit EternalRule.fromKif, reviewed native
Horn/KIF, positive taxonomy review and their authority policies remain separate.
No source approval, conditional applicability or world truth follows from syntax.

Existing real consumers call the repaired signal crossing: text KG intake used

scoped exact identity. No new corpus API or review-admission lane was added.
The uncalled legacy pure reviseInto map fold also refuses crossing/erasing a known
