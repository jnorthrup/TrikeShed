Actual immutable-JAR NARS admission and reinforcement evidence

The v51 baseline and v54 results use frozen production main.jar files identified
by source commit, JAR SHA256, snapshot manifest and classpath hashes in the
identity files. The original baseline artifacts are unchanged. All published
observations here are synthetic. No supplied documents, private corpus text,
provider credentials, model activity or live bag/WAL contents are included.

The same nine baseline cases retain their exact case names and synthetic source,
term and receipt inputs. The v54 driver adds typed per-command refusal capture
and verifies unchanged signal bytes, gloss and budget plus a subsequent processed
fence. It does not treat enqueue success as completed admission. Three v51
failures now refuse explicitly: forced equal angular address with different
ordered roles (STRUCTURE_MISMATCH), null ancestry and empty ancestry
(UNRESOLVED_BASIS). Four supported controls remain supported. The two capability
gaps remain UNSUPPORTED: semantic role querying/variable binding and source
retraction/proof revocation. No fixture labels are inserted as semantic answers.

Four additional controls are separate from the original nine: larger overlapping
claims, zero Bloom hint with an exact shared leaf, actual frame replay through
an in-memory DurableAppendLog, and CAS archive identity/exactly-once revival.
The experiment total is eleven SUPPORTED, zero FAIL and two UNSUPPORTED. The
additional controls retain their preparation label additional-v53-control in raw
JSON; the actual executed artifact was v54, as its identity file records.
Configured capacity was 4096 and intake 256; at most two residents were observed.
This is a counterexample/regression experiment, not a capacity or throughput test.

A separate run compiles only two focused TEST source files (moved to unique
proof.nars package) and a test driver against the same immutable v54 JAR. It
compiles no production sources and uses no friend paths. All eleven methods
pass: seven admission/lifecycle controls, three Reinforce/LCNC controls and one
memory-file feedback control. These eleven tests are not eleven extra reasoning
capabilities and are not added to the experiment case count. Production class
CodeSource checks cover BeliefBagElement, BeliefsNodes and HermesMemoryFiles.
The committed test source hashes and compiled test hashes are recorded separately.
The initial external driver compilation failed on a bracket and a package import;
those fixture-only errors were fixed and the successful second attempt is
recorded. Failed attempt logs remain in the external proof archive.

Reinforce now requires an explicit receipt plus nonempty exact ancestry and uses
the same guarded revision as Mint. Missing fields, unknown/empty ancestry and
post-WAL unknown ancestry cannot add support. Exact receipt replay and overlapping
sources cannot boost evidence even with larger claimed counts. Declared disjoint
positive and negative observations remain usable. LCNC waits for processed
acknowledgment; it reports requested counts rather than claiming every requested
unit was added. Deleting retrieved memory demotes attention without changing
SignalCodec bytes/evidence, and its deleted gloss stays removed after reload.
All isolated bags/workers/supervisors drain and temporary memory files are removed.

The identity guard covers the current signal schema's ordered subject/object,
relation and temporal qualifications. It is not complete predicate/context
identity, a full semantic index, English understanding or role unification. The
equal-angular case is deliberately forced and does not establish a naturally
occurring hash/encoder collision. Source CIDs declare ancestry; they do not
authenticate real-world independence, authority or legal correctness. Existing
unknown history is retained, never silently assigned new ancestry or rolled back.
The WAL fixtures use actual frame encoding/replay with an in-memory log, not a
disk/CRC/process-restart durability proof. Explicit negative storage is distinct
from absence; it does not create a semantic UNKNOWN-answer mechanism.

Both runs used bounded child JVMs with 512m maximum heap and 45s wall timeout,
without a native memory cap or network-denial profile. No models, provider calls,
training, mass seeding or live-state changes occurred. v54 is the measured build;
final v55 rerun evidence will be recorded separately rather than replacing it.
