EnglishLegalPatterns.inspect(source, parsed, begin, end) is a pure, bounded
common helper. It requires parsed.text to exactly equal the selected source
substring and reuses EnglishInspection source/CID, Unicode, graph, and quotation
validation. Returned spans are global UTF16 offsets into that same extracted
text. It calls no model, parser, reasoner, network, or world-fact store.

The output authority is parser-role-and-quantity-candidates-only and its review
status is unreviewed. A modal/action candidate retains the literal modal, its
lemma, the actual dependency edge, and all observed subject/object role nodes.
Enhanced++ node IDs include token index and copy count; copies retain their
original source anchor without inventing extra occurrences of a word.

Duration-language candidates preserve their exact source quotation, number
forms, unit, business/calendar/working modifiers, temporal-marker observations,
and dependency direction. A bounded lexical grammar can read ASCII digits or
ordinary English cardinals from 0 through 9999. Matching thirty (30) may yield
the lexical integer 30; conflicting thirty (60), unsupported number language,
and copied quantity scope remain unresolved. Month/year units are never turned
into days. This is not a legal deadline or a calendar computation.

Every sentence and its lexical guard/exception/negation markers remain visible.
Parser roles, numeric readings, and SUMO/NARS legal facts are different claims:
this helper does not admit facts or establish an obligation, legal force,
negation/exception application, coordination scope, cross-sentence scope, or
parser accuracy. Existing copied/legacy-node inference gates are unchanged.

Additional bounds: 1024 modal candidates, 1024 duration candidates, 32768 emitted
dependency binding references, 24 number-language tokens, 8192 aggregate quoted
layout-anchor references, and 4 MiB canonical CBOR output. EnglishInspection's
source/token/node/edge bounds apply first. Invalid source identities or graphs
are refused; a missing candidate is not evidence that a clause has no effect.
