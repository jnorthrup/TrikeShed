Grant / finance / legal / ledger: next review slice
Read-only source review at master e4b1a1d04 (merged #1077), 2026-11-02.
No Gradle, implementation, financial posting, payment, or external writes.

Recommendation
The next small workflow should compare a selected source budget with source-stated
charges for one grant, line and period, and display an evidence-backed numeric
review report. Call the output a budget cross-reference, not an allowability
finding or a general ledger. Do not implement grant eligibility, journal posting,
or a legal rule engine until actual award/source records are available.

What exists now
* ExactAmount.kt provides CurrencyScale, canonical decimal parsing to checked Long
  minor units, checked addition and exact rendering. JSON boundaries must continue
  to encode units/totals as decimal strings (including values >2^53).
* Reconciliation.kt matches unique account/reference/currency/signed amount/date
  candidates and preserves invalid, duplicate and ambiguous rows. Its source roles
  are caller declarations, not authenticity or approval. Its complete flag is not
  an approval and does not mean every row matched.
* FinanceNodes reads current original ProjectDoc bytes, checks selection equality,
  length, strict UTF-8 and content hash, parses CSV/TSV, records consumed identities,
  then checks both sources again before returning. It reports raw rows, logical
  ordinals, header/cell/record ranges, UTF-16-half-open offset units, parser/policy
  identity and exact string totals. It explicitly does not retain a durable source
  snapshot or obtain an atomic cross-source snapshot.
* Existing finance preset is project.read(bank.csv), project.read(expenditure.csv),
  finance.reconcile, production display. It is executable with a mounted project.
  FinanceKifPresetExecutionTest exercises that graph with synthetic source CSVs.
* ProjectCorpus exposes docs/document/bytes and extraction provenance. LegalNodes
  delegates to canonical document.curate and retains source-linked proposals.
  ConditionSpan/ConditionAssessment can preserve quoted conditions, but they are
  proposed human assessments; authenticated ReviewPrincipal and the private
  CurationReview WAL establish the existing review authority. That machinery is
  not a grant policy resolver, and the strict executable-condition gate is
  currently book-specific.
* docs/grant-finance-ledger-plan.txt is an earlier audit. Its CSV/exact-money
  blocking gaps1/2 are now partly superseded by the merged finance path above.
  Its grant-policy, financial-ledger and financial-approval gaps remain open.

Actual source availability / gaps
The inspected tracked finance inputs are synthetic strings in tests. No actual
award, approved grant budget, fund allocation, accounting journal export, chart of
accounts, or expenditure export with grant/line attribution was identified in the
bounded repository/artifact inventory. The nonprofit demo references a public
IPPF annual report in a separate macOS corpus; an annual report is not an award or
client logframe. Local artifacts include public legal/reference PDFs (including
Coinbase terms), not an identified grant budget or financial journal. The ingest
catalog includes finance textbook paths, not transactional grant data.
No live mounted-project registry was queried; this is not a claim that all user
corpora lack such records. A concrete next implementation needs the selected
ProjectDoc source records/schema, not invented grant data described as real.

Current expenditure schema is only:
  id,account,reference,currency,date,amount
It has no grant/fund/budget-line key, allocation basis, service/incurrence period,
award-version link or allowability evidence. Its amount is a signed source
movement. An outflow must not silently become a positive grant charge by negation.
There is no source-approved legal ruleset, award-amendment precedence, jurisdiction,
period close, restricted-fund policy, or double-entry journal interface established
by this inspection. Runtime Curation/Invocation/Usage ledgers are not financial
journals. A filename, caller policyId, status="approved", or graph label cannot
supply missing authority.

Concrete proposed input contract (NEW schema, not existing real source data)
Two fully selected ProjectDoc inputs, budget and expenditure, current metadata and
original bytes verified as in FinanceNodes. One declared currency+scale and exact
CSV/TSV parser identity; bounded1MiB/4096rows as current finance boundary.

budget.csv required unique headers:
  id,grant_id,budget_line,currency,period_start,period_end,limit_amount,conditions_text
* Unique source id; exact nonblank grant/line keys; ISO dates with start<=end.
* Nonnegative canonical decimal limit_amount parsed without rounding.
* conditions_text is copied exactly, with its cell range. Empty/missing substantive
  terms remain unassessed; empty is never interpreted as unconditional permission.
* Source statement of a limit is not evidence that a funder approved it. A future
  award/amendment link must name retained source versions and exact clause spans.

expenditure.csv: retain the existing six columns and add these required fields:
  grant_id,budget_line,charge_amount
* Existing amount remains the signed movement and remains visible.
* charge_amount is the SOURCE-STATED budget charge, positive cost/negative credit,
  not inferred from the movement sign or supplied by a model. A selected export
  without this field needs an explicit source-backed mapping decision first.
* A negative charge is a stated credit, not proof of a valid refund/reversal.
  Service-period allocation, split charging and credit provenance stay unassessed.
* No fuzzy matching of line names. Date within an inclusive source budget period
  is a numeric cross-reference convention, not a legal conclusion about eligible
  service dates. Overlapping candidate periods are ambiguous, never picked first.

Bounded computation and review output
1. Revalidate both exact selected source heads/bytes. Parse required unique
   headers, quoted cells, logical records and arity with CSVUtil.records. Preserve
   every original cell/range, including unused columns; no trim/locale coercion.
2. For each selected grant+line+currency and unambiguous period, sum charge_amount
   with checked integer arithmetic; compare to the source-stated limit. Use checked
   subtraction too (Long.MIN_VALUE cannot safely be negated). Preserve invalid,
   duplicate, foreign-grant/currency, missing-line and ambiguous-period rows once
   each for review. Do not silently drop them into apparently complete totals.
3. Emit limitMinorUnits, chargeMinorUnits, remainingMinorUnits as strings or null
   when uncomputable, plus numeric comparison and completeness/coverage reasons.
   A value within the stated limit only describes that arithmetic. Always expose
   allowability="unassessed", source roles="caller-selected", and copied conditions.
   Conditions with "only if", exceptions, prior-approval clauses or uncertainty
   must never disappear into a bare eligible=true flag.
4. Include both source CIDs/revisions/sequences/byte lengths, parser identity,
   report-rule version, grant/line/period key and exact row/cell evidence for each
   limit and contributing charge. Repeat current source readback before return.
   Mark current as-of-read scope; do not imply durable retention or atomicity.

  conditions_text="Only with prior written funder consent."

Deferred until real domain records and authority exist
Grant allowability needs an exact award/amendment version, funder/program and
jurisdiction/effective dates, applicable conditions/exceptions, cost/incurrence
facts, supporting evidence and authenticated reviewer scope. Existing quoted
condition/curation machinery may support review after a concrete adapter exists;
parser proposals or council text do not establish those facts. A ledger extension
needs an actual immutable financial journal export and debit/credit/fund/period
semantics; first implement a read-only exact source cross-reference if supplied.
No financial posting/payment, balance-sheet claims, filings or automated approval
is included in this proposed slice.
