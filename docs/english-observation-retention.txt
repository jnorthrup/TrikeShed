Retained NLP observations

DocumentCuratorCodec stores sentence OpenIE relations and KBP tuples, including
all three inclusive token-index ranges, original glosses and finite confidence.
Document mentions retain sentence, inclusive token span, text, chain and
representative flag. Prompt rows declare and preserve the same fields.

Empty ranges remain empty with their original endpoints. An implicit relation's
gloss is an observation, not an invented source token or verified quotation.
Neither a tuple, slot label, mention chain nor confidence establishes legal truth.

Historical records omitted these fields. Their typed read-back uses empty lists
for compatibility and preserves original metadata and reasons. This does not
recover missing outputs or establish that the producer returned none. Inspect
nlpRetentionCoverage on the original retained bytes to distinguish stored field
presence; the helper reports presence only, not validity or annotator execution.
Re-encoding an empty fallback creates present empty fields without recovering
the historical observations. Keep the original receipt and its coverage result.

New present fields with missing members, malformed ranges, wrong types or
nonfinite confidence are refused. Range endpoints are checked as integer pairs;
source-role and semantic validation remain separate. No inference, review
approval or reasoning admission is added by this retention boundary.
