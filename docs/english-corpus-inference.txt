Local English corpus, query and inference boundaries

Documents: open a document, inspect an explicit text range, then use that inspection in the corpus. The project shelf stores retained inspection CIDs, not document text. Build explicitly in current or snapshot mode. Current mode checks the selected document/extraction rows before and after processing; it is not an atomic guarantee of future currency. Snapshot mode reads historical retained content. A current-document link can open a later revision and is labelled accordingly.

The wordcloud displays included lexical lemma counts. Concordance shows exact original sentences and token spans. Original parser words count once; Enhanced++ copy vertices remain graph structure. Disjoint ranges of one original/extraction form one statistical document; duplicate or overlapping ranges and mixed extractions are refused. Punctuation observations remain retained even when excluded from lexical counts. Default policy preserves lexical lemmas without stop-word filtering.

LDA is bounded collapsed-Gibbs sampling with explicit priors, iterations and seed. A repeated model over the same matrix/configuration has the same content identity. Its document/topic counts conserve tokens. Topic mixtures describe the selected vocabulary and ranges; they do not establish comprehension, legal meaning, factual confidence or source independence.

Visual SPARQL selects an explicit retained English observation receipt. Its fresh named graph represents source tokens, copied dependency nodes and lexical markers. Predicate labels describe observations, not asserted legal obligations. The supported basic graph-pattern subset executes over the checked snapshot with source, intermediate-join and response limits. It does not scan a global last-used corpus. CID checks establish retained-byte integrity and source/span consistency, not that an authenticated producer executed the parser or that a reviewer approved the result.

The local LCNC preset carries one receipt CID in an actual Camel direct/mock route message body and EvidenceCid header. A pure check compares routed body, reply, header and expected CID before invoking the same observation-query owner. Camel runs in its installed module classloader. This fixture does not demonstrate a new GraalJS sandbox or remote enterprise endpoints. Its local route is explicit and does not convene a model or admit rules.

CoreNLP 4.6.0 is pinned and installed locally. The six selected SYNTAX model weights match the prior 4.5.10 bundle; fixed-corpus structural comparison and short FULL compatibility runs establish observed compatibility, not improved general English or legal accuracy. FULL records now retain OpenIE, KBP and mention observations with exact ranges, glosses and metadata. Empty observed KBP output is not evidence of nonempty KBP slot coverage.

NARS angular coordinates route attention, not complete proposition identity. Mint checks the available ordered subject/object/relation/temporal structure. New evidence revisions and explicit Reinforce require known, nonempty, disjoint declared leaf ancestry and exact observation receipts. Repeated or overlapping observations do not increase evidence; unknown historical ancestry refuses revision rather than inventing independence. CAS archive revival verifies retained bytes and preserves receipt counting. These guards neither authenticate declared independence nor add missing predicate/context identity, role-based unification or source retraction.

Hermes memory deletion demotes attention and removes its rendered gloss; it is not negative factual evidence. Queued model assertions report queued status until a processed acknowledgment is available. Neither corpus statistics nor query observations automatically enter the bag or Rete.

Coordinated and immutable-artifact proof identities are recorded with the accompanying evidence after verification. Raw supplied PDFs, extracted passages and private-corpus summaries remain outside the repository. Synthetic evidence alone is public.
