Parquet in the document intake
==============================

A .parquet file landed in a project is offered to the curator like a PDF: the miner reads it into the extract twin, book.curate cuts the
extract into sections, constellation.join joins the book. Nothing is OCR'd: the columns are read from the file's own pages.

What is read
------------
borg.trikeshed.parse.parquet (commonMain) reads the footer through a ByteRange, so the byte-range index of every column chunk is the footer's
own (ParquetFooter.chunks: begin, length, codec, values), then the columns asked for, chunk by chunk. Each page is decoded into a SeriesBuffer
that is drained as the chunk's Series; levels and dictionary indices fill an IntColumn. Flat columns; plain, dictionary and (booleans) RLE
pages; data pages v1 and v2. Codecs: UNCOMPRESSED, SNAPPY (Snappy.kt) and ZSTD (tilting/zran) in commonMain, GZIP through the JDK
(ParquetInflate). A repeated column (list, map), another encoding or another codec is refused, never skipped.

What is made of it
------------------
The conventions of the file's notes twin (`<id>.notes.md`, `key: value` lines) name the columns and the rows; nothing is inferred from the data:

  heading: column              the section heading (default: the first text column)
  text: column                 the section body (default: the text column of the most bytes by the footer's sizes)
  where: column=a|b; column=c  keep the rows whose column is one of the values, every condition holding

The extract is `# file`, then `## heading` and the text of each kept row. A selection over 5000 rows is refused, not cut: narrow it with where:.
Put the notes twin before the parquet: the extraction reads it once, when the parquet lands.
